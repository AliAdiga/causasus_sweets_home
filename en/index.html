Our Story

A Caucasian treat with an Amman flavour

At Caucasus Sweets Home we make the classics of Caucasian and Russian cuisine: the Russian cake, honey cake, walnut cake and caramel walnut cookies, along with chocolate balls and custard cups.

Every sweet is made with love, for family gatherings, holidays and those small moments with coffee.
